About

A professional engineer, lifelong programmer.

Programming has been a serious intellectual interest of mine for many years. Although my professional career is in engineering rather than software development, I continue to spend a significant part of my personal time exploring technology.

I enjoy understanding not only how software works, but why different technologies make different trade-offs.

My interests range from expressive languages such as Ruby to systems-oriented languages such as Rust, along with Python, C#, Linux, CLI development, developer tools and AI.

This website is where I document that journey, share things I learn and showcase the projects I build.
